Tunnel Studio 0.21.5
5 November 2026

Local-circle cleaning along a curved tunnel
Clean > Local circles fits circular references perpendicular to a local 3D
centreline. The default fitting length is 5 m, with 50% overlap. Circle offsets
and radii are blended between neighbouring fitting windows instead of cutting
the cloud with unrelated straight cylinders. Choose a scan-derived guide or the
loaded project alignment; neither source coordinates nor the alignment change.

Set separate inward/outward keep distances in millimetres. Preview processes
the whole retained registered cloud without a million-point admission cap.
Optional view filters are off by default. Every original point is classified
in bounded blocks; bounded calibration samples are an internal fitting budget.

Review each fit's radius, support residual and angular coverage. View circle
looks through just its local fitting window; Reverse view looks from the other
end. Viewing direction does not reverse the inward/outward classification.
Green is within band, yellow lacks reference, cyan is a departure candidate,
and orange is explicitly selected for an edit. No candidates are preselected.

Poorly circular, poorly covered or uncertain windows remain unselected. Select
inward, outward or both departure sides, review the original-point counts, then
Remove selected or Separate selected to a new scan. Undo reverses an edit.
Stop and failed saves preserve the previous completed review and source files.

If previous cleaning removed genuine wall points, use Undo or Deleted points
restoration before fitting this reference. A fitted circle is not design control,
semantic service recognition, proof of survey accuracy, or missing-wall repair.
Real non-circular lining and joints require review. The preview does not erase
points automatically. Cumulative from verified 0.21.4, retaining its shape tool,
whole-cloud lining selection, deleted-area restore/export and bundle protection.
