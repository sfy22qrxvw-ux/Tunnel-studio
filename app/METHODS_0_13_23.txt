TUNNEL STUDIO 0.13.23 - METHODS AND VALIDATION
15 October 2026

The accompanying 10-page Tunnel_Studio_Research_Review.pdf contains six full-paper
reviews, equations, measured tests, limitations and references. Implementation
is independent; published accuracy is not claimed or reproduced.

COMBINED DENOISING
Wang T., Wei G., Wang Y. (2024), Journal of Geo-information Science 26(12),
2759-2771; DOI 10.12082/dqxxkx.2024.240505. Chinese full text reviewed.
Stage 1 uses full-density radius support over distinct locations, with a local
neighbour-distance median/MAD filter. Spatial tiles include a 2R halo. Duplicate
coordinates count once for support, but every original point ID is output.
Stage 2 uses at most 140,000 actual coordinates, balanced at 0.01 m voxel scale.
Projected RANSAC circle hypotheses along principal-axis candidates initialise
robust cylinder refinement. This does not reproduce the full adaptive RANSAC
sampler in the paper. Measured mode follows supported local sections and floor
profiles; circular mode classifies with a straight cylinder residual:
  d = norm((p-c) - dot(p-c,n)*n) - r, with norm(n)=1.
The paper's Equation 1 lacks the square on the equivalent axial dot product.
All selected retained points are classified at original XYZ. Labels:
0 lining; 1 sparse; 2 spacing outlier; 3 inward departure; 4 outward departure;
5 insufficient model support. Labels 0 and 5 are automatically retained.
Tolerance defaults to 20 mm and is adjustable 1-500 mm. The paper's 4 mm value,
15 neighbours and 0.1 m radius are dataset settings, not universal choices.
No design radius is used to bias the fit. Strong bends, junctions, missing data
and noncircular tunnels need separate review. Real small details can be flagged.
Detection never deletes points. Review can keep groups or selected points.
Every cleaned output point keeps its measured coordinates and attributes.

CROSS-SECTIONS AND IRREGULARITIES
Shen et al. (2021), DOI 10.11947/j.JGGS.2021.0206, provides relevant robust
section-reference ideas; it does not validate this curved-path implementation.
A locally smoothed centre path and analytic tangent replace one global PCA
normal. Active alignments use local segment vectors including grade. The same
plane defines preview and full-density CSV; abs(dot(p-C,n)) <= thickness/2.
Deleted masks, captured selection and enabled limit box constrain the scope.
Revision tokens cover file identities, sizes/times, counts/bounds, selection,
limit and alignment; stale paths and ellipse fits are rejected.
Irregular detection uses a supported lining reference with a broader longitudinal
median, full-density radial departures and roughness in projected patch cells.
Sensitivity S gives departure threshold 0.12 * 10**(-S/100) m; roughness threshold
is half that. Default pitch 0.20 m, minimum candidate 30 points. Connected
candidates retain original IDs and can be hidden, shown or reversibly deleted.
Unsupported regions and small groups are retained. Source checks and save-failure
rollback protect deletion. It is not an exhaustive defect or design check.

ELLIPSES AND RING PLANES
Wang et al. (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.optlastec.2020.106220, motivates moment frames
and ellipse descriptions. A balanced 2D covariance frame precedes the existing
angular-balanced RANSAC/robust fitter in section_research.py. Calibration uses
the bounded preview; a streaming pass checks every section CSV point. Full
2a >= 2b diameters, inliers and approximate Sampson residuals are explicit.
Near-circular major-axis angle is marked unreliable. Undefined residual gradients
are counted. No original points are moved, filtered or interpolated.
Wang et al. (2025), Applied Sciences 15, 2926, DOI 10.3390/app15062926, motivates
selected-ring covariance/SVD planes. Smallest covariance eigenvector supplies
the normal through the centroid. Spread, rank and angular-gap checks reject
obvious unusable selections. A physical skew ring may differ from the axis-
perpendicular plane. Full GuSAC/TMatch automatic segmentation is not included.

VALIDATION
100 checks in 12 suites passed: 42 backend and 58 production-JavaScript checks
with mocked HTTP/WebGL. See tests/validation_0_13_23.json and result files.
Prior-version tests/logs in tests/ are historical, not the current release gate.
Combined-cleaning synthetic test: 49,740 points; 100% normal lining retained,
99.8667% added stray points flagged, 100% of a constructed 35 mm feature flagged.
The feature result demonstrates why real departures require review. Exact IDs,
coordinates/attributes in exports, order/batch invariance, cancellation,
selection/deletion exclusions and failed-save rollback were checked.
Three analytic tube cases gave maximum normal errors of 0.0625, 0.1597 and
0.0015 degrees. Three constructed irregular patches were found, with point
recall 100%, 100%, 92.46%; regular-point flag rate 0.1412%. Ellipse and rotated
ring checks, full-density CSV membership and source invalidation passed.
These results are not field accuracy or guaranteed defect detection. The full
installed Windows app, installer execution and GPU pixels were not tested here.
The updater performs a component import check in the installed runtime.

MESHING, IMAGERY AND LEICA
TriMe commit 3929c19c7d98a9cee67b3a4e4244659d2d08630f was built separately and
checked on six triangle/annulus runs. It is not linked into Tunnel Studio.
The RL mesher and calibrated stereo densification papers were reviewed; neither
engine was added. Existing tunnel meshing is unchanged. The 0.13.22 direct RTC360
reader is retained with its experimental calibration and validity limitations.
