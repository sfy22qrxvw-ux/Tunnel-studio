Find similar shapes across a registered cloud
Clean > Find similar shapes lets you draw a box, lasso or polygon around one
example: a bolt recess, raised fitting, or short pipe/ventilation section.
Choose the side of the lining and minimum/maximum offset, then find matching
footprints and depth patterns across the whole retained registered scan.

There is no one-million-point admission limit in this tool. All original IDs
are read in blocks of at most 100,000. The drawn example teaches the shape;
it is not the search boundary. Limit boxes and hidden/isolated areas restrict
the search only when Restrict search to current view filters is enabled.

Review candidate groups in orange/cyan, select or deselect matches, and optionally
keep/exclude a drawn area. Remove selected points or Separate selected to new
scan uses full-density original IDs and supports one workspace Undo. Original
XYZ/attribute caches and virtual registration coordinates remain unchanged.
Stop preserves the prior completed result. Failed saves roll back scan edits.

Compact examples compare footprint, edge surroundings and lining-relative depth.
Continuing services may extend beyond the example; connected cells with matching
offsets are followed to their measured ends. Inspect attached branches. Shapes
are geometric candidates, not confirmed semantic identities or probabilities.
Unsupported lining areas remain unselected. Missing wall is not reconstructed.

The method independently adapts height/template matching and lining-offset ideas
from the uploaded Shi et al. (2021) and Zhang et al. (2026) papers. It does not
include a trained PointNet++ model or inherit the papers' accuracy claims.

Cumulative from 0.21.3, including whole-cloud above/below-lining selection,
deleted-area restore/export, and independent registered-cloud preservation.
