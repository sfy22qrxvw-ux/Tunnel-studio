Tunnel Studio 0.17.2 - measured surfaces and triangle meshes

WHAT A COMPLETED MESH CONTAINS
A mesh contains shared vertices and triangular faces. Neighbouring faces use
the same vertex indices along their shared edges. A filled triangle surface is
different from a point cloud, even when both describe the same shape.

A useful tunnel mesh may have open portals, measurement boundaries and genuine
openings. Such a surface can be connected without enclosing a watertight volume.
The words solid display describe filled triangle rendering; they do not certify
a closed CAD solid, lining thickness, or a valid enclosed volume.

CREATE TUNNEL SURFACE
This is the primary direct lining-mesh workflow for one approximately straight
tunnel branch. It uses a shared-vertex radial grid fitted to retained measured
points. It is appropriate after selecting the branch and reviewing or cleaning
services, equipment and other geometry that should not become part of the lining.
Use the alignment-grid workflow for a curved tunnel with a suitable alignment.
Separate junctions and overlapping branches before fitting one radial surface.

The desktop path reads the original float64 coordinate cache in bounded blocks.
Deleted points are excluded by the current mask. A supplied selection and active
limit box constrain the source area. The display point cache is not the meshing
input, and meshing does not write an index beside the original source scan.

The first source pass obtains retained bounds and stable centred statistics.
The next pass assigns every retained finite measurement in the chosen area to
an axial/angular cell. Support counts are exact. A small, bounded reservoir of
measured radii supplies each cell's fitted median. Original point identities
determine reproducible reservoir priorities independently of input chunk size.

For a cell whose measurements fit in its reservoir, the median is exact. For a
cell with more measurements, the reported radius is an approximate median of
the retained reservoir. Quality controls the reservoir capacity; the overall
grid budget can reduce that capacity. The saved mesh summary records the
effective capacity, exact and approximate cell counts, source passes, measured
support and effective grid dimensions. Reading all retained support does not
mean keeping every source point in memory or computing every median exactly.

The grid and working arrays have automatic size controls. Source point count
still affects reading and processing time. These controls do not establish a
universal runtime or whole-application memory guarantee for every possible
project, imported model, or combination of open tools.

Cell vertices are fitted positions, not unchanged scan returns. Adjacent
supported cells are triangulated with shared indices and consistent radial
winding. Edge limits prevent overly long connections. A request that produces
no supported faces fails with an explanation rather than publishing a vertex
cloud labelled as a mesh.

PHYSICAL GAP LIMITS AND OPENINGS
Small-gap interpolation uses only original measured support in a single pass.
An empty cell must have admissible support in both the angular and axial
directions. Angular distance uses the local radius, and the combined tangential
or axial span and radial change must fit inside the stated physical gap limit.
Testing these distances together prevents a large diagonal jump from passing
two separate small-coordinate tests.

Newly interpolated cells are not reused to grow repeatedly across an opening.
An unmeasured complete ring, a long slit, a large hole or a portal is therefore
not filled merely because an individual cross-gap distance looks short.
Increasing cell size changes resolution and the amount of support per cell;
it does not increase the chosen physical gap limit.

Faces using interpolated cells are marked as inferred geometry for review.
Separate hole-repair commands retain their existing portal, extent, topology
and overlap checks. A capped or repaired result is not automatically certified
as a watertight survey volume.

FLOAT64 COORDINATES AND GRID BOUNDARY TIES
Original survey coordinates and saved mesh vertices remain float64. Local
centred coordinates are used for sensitive geometric calculations and display.
Near an existing cell boundary, the grid resolves floating-point ties using a
roundoff allowance derived from coordinate magnitude and projection scale.
The PCA unwrap path also uses stable bin ties and local-coordinate averaging.

This allowance is numerical uncertainty, not a survey accuracy setting, a
welding tolerance or permission to fill a physical gap. It addresses artificial
alternating empty rows or angular cells caused by roundoff at regular sampling
boundaries. A nearly circular cross-section uses a stable transverse frame when
its radial eigenvalues do not determine a unique orientation. A measurement
within the numeric tie interval can be assigned to a neighbouring existing bin;
arbitrary translations are not promised to produce bitwise-identical fitted
medians. Requests below reliable float64 bin resolution are rejected.

OTHER MESH METHODS
Generic methods obtain spatially distributed measured representatives through
bounded source passes. A representative is an unchanged measured XYZ row near
its voxel centre. This avoids allocating most working points to the densest
scanner region simply because that region occupies most input rows. Working
budgets and effective sampling resolution are reported automatically.

Scan to Mesh Auto selects a supported reconstruction route from the selected
geometry and reports its choice. PCA tunnel unwrap, planar Delaunay, Ball
Pivoting, Screened Poisson, Alpha Shape, explicit Convex Hull and voxel/Marching
Cubes remain separate methods with different assumptions. A planar projection
is not a general reconstruction of a tunnel. A convex hull is an outer envelope
that can span concavities. Normal-dependent engines need suitable oriented
normals, and implicit reconstruction can produce geometry in weakly supported
areas. The method's edge limits and review information still matter.

Spatial sampling changes the relative influence of densely repeated returns.
A lower point-weighted distance to an uncleaned cloud is not, by itself, proof
of a better lining surface. Small interior objects can affect that metric even
when the intended output is the surrounding tunnel. No meshing method here is
a semantic guarantee that pipes, cables, bolts or services have been removed.

RESULT QUALITY AND CONNECTIVITY
The result summary distinguishes triangles, edge-connected pieces, the largest
piece's area fraction, boundary edges, non-manifold edges, isolated triangles
and winding conflicts. Touching at one vertex does not count as a joined sheet.
All output components are retained; small pieces are not silently discarded to
make a connectivity statistic look better.

For the radial grid, measured-cell retention reports how many observed cells
contribute to at least one face. It does not measure completion of unobserved
areas. The report also records how many candidate triangles an edge limit
removed. Fragmented or poorly supported output is labelled for review rather
than described as a complete continuous lining.

Triangle shape quality uses Q = 4*sqrt(3)*area / sum(edge_length_squared).
An equilateral triangle has Q=1; a degenerate sliver approaches zero. A high Q
does not prove source accuracy, correct classification or complete coverage.
Boundary and edge-incidence checks do not certify absence of self-intersections
or a watertight volume.

The built-in fit diagnostic compares area-weighted mesh samples with bounded
spatial representatives of retained measurements. Its normalized bidirectional
squared Chamfer value is an approximate sampled comparison. It is not exact
distance to all triangle interiors, an independent full-source validation, or
a survey acceptance criterion. The release's independent real-scan checks use
withheld source points and nearest triangle-interior distances separately.

TRIANGLE DISPLAY
The result card offers Solid, Solid + wire and Wire display, source visibility,
preview retry and full-mesh export. It distinguishes saved triangle counts from
displayed counts. Point-cloud colour and point-size choices do not replace the
mesh's triangle representation.

Large previews use connected surface simplification within the display budget.
They do not randomly sample unrelated faces. A simplified preview leaves the
saved full mesh unchanged. A failed triangle preview is shown as an error with
its reason and a retry route; there is no silent vertex-only mesh fallback.
When one model fails in a mixed scene, usable models can still display and the
scene reports a partial error. Display failure and incomplete reconstructed
geometry are different states, and both remain visible to the user.

SAVE, EXPORT AND CANCELLATION
Mesh creation and refinement write assets to a private operation directory.
Before publication, the source geometry and exclusion-mask revision are checked
again. A complete model is added to the workspace through the project save.
Cancellation, a changed source or a failure before commit removes only that
operation's unpublished assets and leaves the previous workspace intact.
The last cancellable checkpoint precedes publication; a completed committed
model is not subsequently reported as an unpublished cancelled operation.

OBJ export reads the full saved vertex and face caches in bounded blocks. It
exports every saved triangle, not the simplified screen preview. Vertex text
uses 17 significant digits so an OBJ reader using float64 can recover the saved
coordinate values. OBJ face groups distinguish original_surface from
inferred_repair faces where that provenance is available.

The OBJ writer validates cache lengths and face indices, writes to a temporary
file beside the destination, and replaces the destination only after a complete
write. A failed write removes the temporary file. Cancelling the save dialog
publishes no export. Other formats retain their existing capability notices;
this change does not make every format preserve scanner setups, panoramas or
every point-cloud attribute.

RESEARCH MAPPING
The supplied Isotropic Point Cloud Meshing using unit Spheres (IPCMS), original
2023 version by Lipschuetz, Reitebuch, Polthier and Skrodzki, is a different
reconstruction algorithm. Its oriented splats, unit-sphere candidate placement,
priority queues, half-edge region bookkeeping, crossing checks and region
triangulation are not implemented here as the complete IPCMS method.

This release applies relevant principles about sufficient local support,
connectivity, controlled openings and triangle-quality reporting. The Q metric
above is the metric discussed on IPCMS page 10. Relevant method and limitation
sections are pages 3-9 and 13. The original version is arXiv:2305.07570v1; a later
extended version has a different title and must not be confused with the
user-supplied original method.

The supplied triangle.pdf is Point2Mesh: A Self-Prior for Deformable Meshes,
Hanocka, Metzer, Giryes and Cohen-Or, 2020, arXiv:2005.11084. Its actual method
uses a deformable triangular mesh and a per-input MeshCNN/self-prior optimization
with coarse-to-fine refinement and a beam-gap loss. This application does not
implement that complete neural reconstruction. Area-weighted triangle sampling
from its section 3.2 informs the fit diagnostic; using that metric is not a
Point2Mesh implementation. The paper's closed-mesh prior also does not establish
that a tunnel should have its portals sealed.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
See VALIDATION_0_17_2.json for final release checks and source hashes. The source
evidence work/validation_0_17_2/real_comparison.json records the controlled
baseline/candidate real-scan runs, unchanged-source hashes, withheld queries,
settings, connectedness, coverage, resource observations and fit measures.
Use the final bound records rather than an archived intermediate iteration.

The available raw scan has no labelled lining/service ground truth and has
strongly nonuniform density. Whole-area fitting can retain sparse disconnected
pieces even when it recovers more measured surface. Coarser detail can improve
support while reducing local detail. No universal single-solid outcome or
semantic-cleaning accuracy is claimed. The user's full 550-million-point cloud
and native Windows/WebView2 execution were not benchmarked on the Linux host.
