How this works · learn the method

GuSAC and TMatch: GuSAC uses regional robust plane fits and sign-independent normal agreement. TMatch groups connected lining points, with optional supported ring grouping on geometric station. This is an implementation based on Wang et al. (2025), DOI 10.3390/app15062926, with documented corrections to the printed normal equations and explicit choices for unspecified parameters. The original coordinates remain unchanged. Reported paper accuracy is not a validation result for this scan. See GUSAC_TMATCH_METHOD_0_15_0.txt for the implementation and test scope.

Noise removal: first checks for a minimum number of distinct neighbouring locations inside a physical radius. It then compares mean neighbour distance with a local median/MAD threshold. Larger multipliers retain more points. Overlapping spatial regions provide complete support across processing boundaries. Duplicate coordinates do not count as extra support. See CLEAN_TUNNEL_METHOD_0_12_0.txt for equations.

SMRG surface extraction:

  1. Normals and patches: local PCA estimates point normals. Connectivity combines normal difference, distance and surface offset to form supervoxels. Density clustering splits detached pieces.
  2. Connections: neighbouring patches form a graph. Line or plane fits and a median absolute deviation test remove unusual links.
  3. Region growing: patch saliency determines seed order and which normal, offset and convexity tests can join regions.
  4. Surface recovery: the largest region is the starting guess. Small regions whose hull boundaries sufficiently overlap it can be added.
  5. Your review: inspect what was kept and missed, change cluster decisions, then export or apply. Closely attached cables and incomplete scans can defeat geometry-only separation.

Reading the result: processed means original retained points in your chosen area. Green means points assigned to Tunnel / keep. Red means all points assigned to Noise / remove, including rejected pieces and density noise. Magenta means selected for editing; selecting alone never changes a piece’s group. Automatic removal may include real tunnel points, so review it. Patch resolution is a grouping scale, not a guaranteed accuracy.

Implementation choices: the paper does not fully specify patch resolution, density parameters or all graph details. The default uses boundary-point adjacency at twice local spacing. A literal centroid-neighbour mode is included for comparison. Equation 19 is directional as printed; the symmetric variant is separately labelled. Density minimum 3, radius multiplier 2.5, refinement limit 3 and occupied-grid target count are our choices. Full details: SURFACE_METHOD_0_4_0.txt in the update.

Based on Song, Jin, Ji & Chen (2026), Supervoxel-based multi-level region growing method for surface point cloud extraction in intersecting tunnels, Scientific Reports, DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-73755-6. Paper: CC BY 4.0. Independent implementation; not the authors’ code or an author-validated reproduction.

    Equations and interpretation

    Circular design: find the closest 3D centreline segment and interpolate its chainage and radius. d = √(right² + up²) − R. Positive is outside; negative is inside. This is radial deviation, not shortest distance to a curved or tapered design surface.

    Triangle design: q is the nearest point on any design triangle; d = ‖p − q‖. Optional sign follows (p − q) · n, where n is that triangle's normal. Verify the normal direction; this is not a closed-volume inside/outside test. Undefined tangential signs at open edges are excluded.

    Statistics: mean = Σd / N; RMS = √(Σd² / N). Within tolerance means |d| ≤ tolerance. P95 absolute is the 95th percentile of |d|. The comparison band excludes large deviations from statistics; excluded counts remain visible and in the CSV.

    Understand every result

    01 / Chainage and section

    Each scan point is projected to the nearest segment of your alignment in plan. Chainage is interpolated between your supplied station labels. The selected section uses a plane perpendicular to the local 3D alignment tangent. Right and up are relative to that plane.

    The alignment is a polyline approximation. Supply sufficiently dense points on curves; exact curves, spirals and station equations are not implemented. The frame uses the forward segment at a vertex.

    02 / Design deviation

    For a circular design: deviation = √(right² + up²) − design radius. Positive is outside the design surface; negative is inside. The wall band removes points farther than the selected amount from that surface. It may also remove real defects: review it carefully.

    All retained slice points contribute to deviation statistics. Display sampling never changes those statistics.

    03 / Ovality

    One median-radius observation per occupied 1° bin balances dense and sparse parts of the scan. A least-squares circle gives the centre offset. An ellipse uses a normalised implicit residual; it is not an orthogonal-distance or claimed ITA-certified implementation.

    Ovality (%) = 100 × (major diameter − minor diameter) / design diameter. Diameters are through the fitted ellipse centre. Fits are withheld with fewer than 12 occupied bins or an angular gap above 60°.

    04 / Ring creep

    Creep (mm) = 1000 × (measured chainage − design chainage). Positive is ahead; negative is behind. Each row must refer to the same stated ring face in design and survey.

    You may supply a measured face chainage directly or E/N/H of a defined check point. Point projection does not automatically fit a ring-face plane. Ring taper and intended face position belong in the design chainage. Joint suggestions remain separate: keeping a candidate is a visual review, not a measured or approved creep result.

    Registration and exchange

    Target fitting supports known-diameter spheres and black-and-white HDS targets. Sphere fitting is assisted: pick a measured point on the sphere and enter its physical diameter; Tunnel Studio robustly fits the centre while keeping the nominal radius fixed. B&W fitting can use a known printed size or automatic multi-scale contrast search. Accepted B&W crossings are checked against original-density points in all four quadrants. Detailed view and screen density are independent from the full-density local fit. Equal-weight rigid least squares uses at least 3 non-collinear target pairs; independent check targets do not influence the fit.

    Neutral scan exports: unstructured E57 (double XYZ), LAS/LAZ (0.1 mm XYZ quantisation), PLY, PTS, XYZ and CSV. E57 preserves complete intensity channels; RGB is mapped to 8-bit. LAS/LAZ and PTS scale intensity as stated in the export notes. No native 3DR, LGS/LGSx, RAF, RCP or scanner-setup database is written. Leica import support depends on product version and licence. Confirm a small exchange sample before a large delivery.
