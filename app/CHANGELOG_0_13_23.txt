Tunnel Studio 0.13.23 - Combined denoising and local cross-sections

- Model > Combined denoising combines full-density neighbour support and robust
  lining fitting. Choose a measured profile or a straight circular cylinder.
- Adjustable lining tolerance in mm; separate red review groups for isolated
  points and lining departures. Uncertain model regions remain retained.
- Cleaned coordinates and attributes remain tied to original point IDs.
- Optional Fit ellipse outline overlays the measured section and reports full
  diameters plus full-density residual statistics; the CSV stays unchanged.
- Selected ring plane fits a plane to one explicit narrow ring selection.
  It does not implement automatic GuSAC/TMatch segmentation.
- Cross-sections now follow a cached local 3D tunnel tangent or the active
  alignment's local segment, including grade, instead of one global PCA normal.
- Robust local quadratic centre smoothing avoids flattening curved path ends.
  The browser cut and full-density export use the same path/frame definition.
- Selected regions, deletion masks and enabled limit boxes constrain the guide
  and section. Changed source/alignment/selection rejects stale cached paths.
- Model > Irregular surfaces finds connected protrusions, recesses and rough
  patches. Sensitivity, patch size and minimum count are adjustable.
- Full-density original IDs support per-patch inspection, preview Hide/Show,
  selected-candidate deletion, updated counts, global Undo and Deleted restore.
- Candidate representatives are included in previews so small detected patches
  do not disappear just because of a regular display stride.
- Deletion validates source revision and rolls back state/files on a failed save.
- Read all six supplied papers, including the Chinese denoising paper, checked latest RL preprint and TriMe source,
  and built/tested TriMe separately. No new mesh engine is shipped in this update.
- Keeps 0.13.22 experimental direct RTC360 import and its recorded limitations.

Validation is synthetic/backend and production JS with mocked HTTP/WebGL.
No Windows/GPU execution, field defect accuracy or survey accuracy claim.
