TUNNEL STUDIO - SIX-PAPER ALGORITHM REVIEW
30 October 2026 | Baseline 0.18.0 | Resulting maintenance release 0.18.1

DECISION
Retain the current combination of reviewed regional GuSAC cleaning, local 3D
section frames, angular-balanced robust ellipse fitting, and explicit design or
measured-point distance calculations. The newly supplied 2025 displacement
paper does not establish a superior replacement for that combination. Its ring
organization and reporting ideas are useful; its printed filtering rule and
unrestricted ring-wise alignment must not become monitoring defaults.

There is no demonstrated universal best algorithm across cleaning, section
extraction, comparison to design and change between epochs. These tasks measure
different things. The software must identify the quantity actually calculated,
preserve observations and retain the limits of each method.

WERE THE TWO ATTACHED PAPERS ALREADY USED?
1. Camara, Wang and You (2024), Sensors 24, 7192, DOI 10.3390/s24227192:
   yes. section_research.py and SECTION_METHOD_0_12_0.txt explicitly cite it.
   The archived 0.17.12 source already includes the citation, corrected ellipse
   denominator, robust fitting and common-origin radial comparison. This
   finding is based on code, not an assumption from the filename.
2. Camara, Wang and You (2025), Applied Sciences15,625,
   DOI 10.3390/app15020625: no implementation of this complete pipeline or exact
   citation was found in the current app. Related distances, registration and
   section metrics already exist. The exact paper has now been reviewed in
   full. Source absence does not prove it was never mentioned in an unavailable
   historical conversation.

COMPARISON WITH THE EARLIER PAPERS

Shen et al. 2021 - dense TLS cross-sections and convergence
DOI 10.11947/j.JGGS.2021.0206. Useful contributions are local section coordinates,
robust median/MAD residual screening and explicit chord/convergence definitions.
The experiment uses horizontal PCA for a linear circular tunnel, two internal
geometric reference locations, 720 angular sectors, floor removal and circle
fitting. The internal locations are not surveyed control targets. The authors
explicitly discuss the need to segment curves rather than use whole-tunnel 2D
PCA (PDF p.14). Their 1.34 mm result belongs to their test and reference data.
Decision: retain local 3D perpendicular frames and reviewable residual detection.
Do not make global2D PCA, universal0.5-degree sectors, automatic invert removal,
or forced circle agreement the default for curved/graded RTC360 jobs.

Wang et al. 2020 - fractional smoothing,3D moments and ellipse extraction
DOI 10.1016/j.optlastec.2020.106220. Useful contributions are local orientation,
moments and best-fit ellipse description. The paper subdivides its roughly 64 m
tunnel into near-straight portions, applies 5 x 5 fractional smoothing and uses
point interpolation (PDF pp.1-5,8-9).
Decision: retain local supported frames and robust ellipse outlines. Do not
replace measured survey observations with smoothed/interpolated coordinates for
deformation measurements. Current free-section ellipse outlines are calibrated
on a bounded preview and checked against all extracted CSV rows; that outline
fit must not be described as a full-point exact optimization.

Wang, Wei and Wang 2024 - orthogonal rotation and combined denoising
DOI 10.12082/dqxxkx.2024.240505. Useful contributions are support filtering,
robust cylinder fitting and section orientation. Their stated assumptions
include short straight regular cylindrical tunnels without major dents. They
use 10 mm voxels, tune a 4 mm cylinder threshold on their data partly against design
radius, and resample fitted circles. They explicitly discuss noncircular and
missing-data limitations (PDF pp.3,5-7,11).
Decision: keep combined denoising as a reviewed alternative; retain source
coordinates and true local 3D frames. Do not copy the 4 mm threshold universally,
snap lining onto a cylinder, or let closeness to design define measurement truth.

Wang et al. 2025 - GuSAC/TMatch segmentation and ring sections
DOI 10.3390/app15062926. Useful contributions are regional RANSAC planes, normal
agreement, sliding local support, lining components/GMM and ring-plane fitting.
This is the most directly applicable of these papers to the requested removal
of interior clutter while retaining supported lining. The implementation is an
independent interpretation with documented corrections to inconsistent printed
normal equations. It classifies original retained points, protects uncertain
lining and joints, and makes exclusions reviewable. TMatch-style connectivity
is exact on eligible original points; named results and model calibration are
bounded. The authors' underspecified crown/MST axis and some reassignment steps
are not reproduced exactly. Their F1=.9527 and IoU=.9055 are their ten-ring
classification results, not a Tunnel Studio field accuracy or service-removal
guarantee. Local normalized CD/RMSE values in the paper are unitless.
Decision: retain GuSAC-based cleaning as the primary current route; retain
TMatch as geometric instance grouping, not pipe/cable semantic recognition.

Camara et al.2024 - robust ellipse and section-deformation descriptors
DOI 10.3390/s24227192. Useful quantities include eccentricity, centre movement,
axis changes, orientation and radial comparison. Current fitting balances
angular samples, uses RANSAC plus robust geometric refinement, withholds weak
coverage and undefined near-circular orientation, and evaluates residual support
on retained inputs. Full-point processing does not mean every original point
has equal weight in the fitted ellipse: the fit uses 1-degree balanced bins.
The printed p.9 Eq.4 uses a in both denominators; the code correctly uses a,b.
The p.7 standard-deviation expressions are inconsistent; p.10 health-score
normalization is unspecified. The p.11 radial expression is shape-only because
it omits centre offsets; the code separately retains common-origin radial
change and centre movement. The paper's small inter-instrument differences do
not establish an RTC360 uncertainty budget or a structural health percentage.
Decision: retain the corrected descriptors, robust fitting and common frame.
Do not copy the uncalibrated health score or transfer claimed sub-mm accuracy.

Camara et al.2025 - ring-wise displacement workflow
DOI 10.3390/app15020625. The analysed experiment covers 102 rings / approximately 122 m,
two epochs six months apart, with a rail-mounted SILSS compared to Pegasus.
It manually crops the facility-heavy lower lining, applies a neighbour-distance
filter, detects seams, aligns corresponding rings with ICP, calculates unsigned
nearest-point distances and derives centroid/PCA-based shape summaries.
Decision: accept physical-ring identity, epoch organization, trends and separate
centre/shape maps as useful workflow ideas. Do not adopt the complete pipeline
as the new measurement default for static RTC360 setups.

WHY THE 2025 DISPLACEMENT PIPELINE IS NOT A SAFE LITERAL REPLACEMENT
1. Its p.6 Eqs.1-3 define mean neighbour distance, then print T=mean-3*SD and
   remove points below T. That rejects unusually dense observations, not sparse
   distance outliers. The minus sign and inequality were checked visually.
   A typo or different unpublished implementation is possible; the printed
   rule is not an appropriate implementation specification.
2. Free rigid ICP on each ring can remove real translation/rotation before
   residuals are calculated. That can answer shape change after best-fit
   alignment, but does not establish absolute motion. Registration against
   stable independent control must remain separate from the movement measured.
3. Nearest scan points need not be the same material point. Their Euclidean
   distance is nonnegative and affected by sampling. It is not a signed
   inward/outward change, a 3D displacement vector or an uncertainty estimate.
4. A point centroid and PCA axes depend on density and visible coverage.
   Removing the invert or seeing different parts of the lining can move the
   centroid and change covariance with no actual deformation. Robust geometric
   fitting with coverage checks is preferable to that substitution here.
5. Reported 0.02 mm detection/precision is not established by independent known-
   motion truth. Agreement between two systems processed by the same workflow
   can share biases. A repeated no-motion baseline, independent checks and an
   error/uncertainty model are needed before choosing a detection threshold.

CONTROLLED METHOD CHECKS RUN DURING THIS REVIEW
These deterministic Linux examples test mathematical consequences and current
code. They are not reproductions of a paper's field experiment or RTC360
accuracy specifications.
- Same 3 m radius circular surface sampled at different angular phases: true
  surface change 0 mm; current exact nearest-point distance mean 1.308997 mm.
  This validates the distance calculation and demonstrates its interpretation.
- A true 10 mm rigid movement followed by free best-fit translation: residual
  below 1e-9 mm. The movement has been absorbed by the fit.
- Current robust section comparison on a known +10 mm right / -6 mm up translation:
  recovered 9.999999 mm / -5.999999 mm, with effectively unchanged axes.
- Unchanged circle with 40 degrees of coverage missing: raw point centroid
  shifts 367.430838 mm; robust fitted centre stays effectively unchanged while
  sufficient angular support remains. This ideal case is not an occlusion-
  independent accuracy guarantee.
- The new application regression additionally preserves +15 mm right / -7 mm up
  across different epoch source/mask metadata and verifies original-file SHA-256.

APPLIED FIX IN0.18.1
Saved measured-section comparisons now require the same plane, slice width,
wall band, design radius and fit threshold. Previously, only plane and width
were checked. A change in filtering/fitting settings could therefore appear
as a change in shape. The guard runs before the ellipse fit and provides the
saved values needed to restore a comparable calculation. Incomplete legacy
baselines require recreation; missing settings are not guessed. Different
epoch source files and masks remain allowed. No coordinates are transformed
by this guard. Existing0.18.0 registration, whole-project analysis, delete,
preview and storage fixes are retained.

REMAINING MONITORING GAP
Current Cloud vs Cloud is exact unsigned nearest measured-point distance. A
separate confidence-qualified epoch engine is still absent. A strong candidate
for development is signed local surface-normal change with support/occlusion
checks and a spatial detection limit. M3C2-EP is relevant when measurement and
alignment uncertainties can actually be supplied: Winiwarter, Anders and
Hoefle 2021, DOI 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2021.06.011. The authors' framework propagates
measurement/alignment uncertainty, including multiple setups. It is not
implemented here and has not been benchmarked against these RTC360 jobs.
It estimates normal change; a full3D material displacement vector additionally
needs persistent correspondences/targets or identifiable features. Smooth
cylindrical geometry alone cannot resolve every tangential or axial motion.

WHAT WOULD ESTABLISH THE BEST DEFAULT FOR THESE JOBS
Use the same representative straight/curved/graded and cluttered RTC360 data
for candidates. Independently control the datum and reserve check observations
outside fitting. Include repeat scans with no movement; known translations,
rotations and radial changes; changed sampling/occlusion; real joints and
lining behind pipes; and the same physical rings across epochs. Compare bias,
repeatability, false positives, retained lining, missed movement, support gaps,
runtime, peak memory and temporary disk. Validate at realistic full-tunnel
scale. Choose settings on training/calibration areas and assess separate held-
out areas. A smaller fit RMS or cleaner-looking cylinder alone is not proof.

VALIDATION SCOPE
233 application tests passed with no failures/errors/skips after this change;
156 Python files parsed and 47 JavaScript files syntax checked. The Windows
package receives separate activation and archive/hash checks. No native
Windows run, full user tunnel field test, reproduced publication accuracy,
universal7 GB memory guarantee or automatic safety certification is claimed.
