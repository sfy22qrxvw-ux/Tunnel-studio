TUNNEL STUDIO 0.14.2 — 16 October 2026

CLEARER MODELLING
- Primary commands have visible short names alongside distinct symbols.
- Track midpoint: choose Circle, hover over one corner until marker 1 appears,
  then the opposite corner until marker 2 appears. Click the centre marker.
- Opposite corners on the same planar model face give its exact midpoint and
  lock the new circle to that face, including vertical and tilted faces.
  Two arbitrary points alone do not define a plane: the current work plane is
  used if there is no common face. The prompt makes this explicit.
- Model view shows editable models separately. Clean/Edit/Analyse show scan
  work without those model overlays. Each view remembers its camera.
  Enable Cloud reference in Model when you deliberately need scans/reference
  meshes. Right-click a cloud > Work in Model mode enables this reference.
- Hidden models cannot be picked, exported as the current highlight or removed
  through the scan view. Model/cloud exchange and destination bundles remain.
- Corrected drawing prompts and incomplete circle previews. Lines/circles drawn
  on solid faces remain visible without flickering into the face.
- Existing visual extrusion, modeless settings, Escape and separate tool windows
  are retained. Face extrusion creates a new body, not a push/pull edit.

CLEANING
- Standard Extract tunnel surface behavior is preserved as the default.
- Clean tunnel > Interior cleanup > More thorough checks additional inward
  remnants, including floating points in limited areas of missing wall support.
  Extra interior candidates have their own review group.
- Near-wall candidates require two lining references to agree. The inward keep
  band widens where voxel calibration or the angular profile is coarse, helping
  preserve flat floors and real wall points behind or between pipes.
- Find reflection artefacts adds a separate conservative detector. It combines
  supported lining departure with approximate mirrored geometry, a known scanner
  position and available intensity. Several cues must agree; weak evidence is
  retained. Intensity alone does not remove points and missing data is not guessed.
- Review the preview, keep real features you need, then create/export the cleaned
  scan or apply the reviewed result. Every selected original point is classified;
  original scan files and coordinates are not overwritten.

CHECKED AND LIMITED
Numerical, production UI and actual Chromium/WebGL checks cover the changed
workflows. Synthetic cleaning cases include flat/rotated noisy floors, sparse
wall behind pipes, floating remnants, mirrored ghosts and real outer recesses.
The two supplied output screenshots were inspected; their original XYZ was not
available. No field accuracy or zero-lag guarantee is implied. Windows/WebView2
and physical multi-monitor behavior were not run on the user's computer.
