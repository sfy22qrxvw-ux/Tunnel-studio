Tunnel Studio 0.18.8
2026-11-02

Manual visual registration
- Registration > Visual alignment opens a fixed blue / moving orange pair.
- Switch between plan, side elevation, front elevation and 3D inspection while retaining the draft alignment.
- Drag, rotate about a chosen pivot, and use millimetre/degree steps with local Undo, Redo and Reset.
- Elevation starts in height-only mode; tilt correction requires explicitly unlocking tilt.
- Join and lock saves a rigid pose for the connected moving group. The fixed group and original point coordinates are preserved. Workspace Undo restores the previous registration.
- Manual links are identified as manual and do not report an invented fit RMS.

Mesh repair
- Selected CAD surfaces and imported meshes reach the repair dialog correctly, including after switching ribbon tabs.
- The dialog offers an explicit source chooser when selection is empty or ambiguous.
- Changing source clears stale inspection data. Repair creates a separate copy and supports Undo.

Bolt-hole point selection
- Selecting a detected bolt-recess guide selects its labelled original cloud points for review and deletion.
- Ctrl-selection and select-all combine recess groups; deletion operates on original point IDs and supports Undo.
- Preview limits are disclosed separately from the full selected point count. The detection algorithm is unchanged.

Live point-to-plane measurements
- The transparent cyan plane appears after the third valid reference point.
- Point placement and coordinate edits update perpendicular distance automatically.
- Hover snaps to visible displayed scan points; a confirmed pick recovers the original precise coordinate.
- Saved measurement planes remain filled and transparent after reopening the workspace.

Cumulative release
- Includes the 0.18.7 deletion, retained-result and setup-view theme changes.
- Uses the verified 0.18.7 bundled Windows runtime and transactional installer lifecycle.
- Existing projects and scans are kept; prior active program files remain available for rollback.
