Tunnel Studio 0.21.2
4 November 2026

Completed registered clouds remain after source-bundle deletion
The Project Explorer keeps a materialised Registered cloud outside its source
bundle. Older projects are corrected when their item inventory is read. Source
setups remain accessible under the joined-scan bundle, with their individual
setup views, target links and visibility controls.

Delete source bundle reviews the original setups and explicitly lists any
Registered cloud that will be kept. The deletion transaction preserves the
stored merged cloud, independent cleaned clouds, point-edit masks, meshes and
saved analyses that remain in the project. Source provenance records report
removed setups without retaining obsolete source-cache paths as dependencies.
A result that the user explicitly selects can still be deleted separately.

Show, Hide, Fit and Export of a source bundle operate on its source setups.
The registered output and original setups remain alternative views of the
same overlapping observations. Point coordinates and counts are unchanged by
these explorer grouping and visibility controls.
