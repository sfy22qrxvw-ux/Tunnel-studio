CACHE AND STORAGE
Two exact hash-threshold levels store original float64 XYZ, float32 attributes and int64 IDs. First level is read alone when sufficient; second is read only if needed.
Final preparation: 47.040 s; 1,152,125,760 bytes (1.15 GB) for both levels. Hard cap: 1,296,000,000 bytes, plus small metadata; one pair per source.
Cache preparation streams the existing XYZ/attribute files once. No duplicate full cloud. It is independent of pose and deletion edits; current masks and Hide/Isolate are applied on every read.
Revision validation rejects stale geometry/attributes. Cancellation is checked between bounded blocks. Cache files have source ownership and are included in scan cleanup.
Large newly prepared scans build this accelerator during preview preparation. Existing scans need one preparation pass; it was performed in the separate test workspace here. No installer migration is applied to the original survey.
An initial 384 MB-only experiment retained exactness but fell back too slowly at the tightest views; it was replaced, not accumulated. A temporary-filename collision during initial preparation was fixed before the final run.
