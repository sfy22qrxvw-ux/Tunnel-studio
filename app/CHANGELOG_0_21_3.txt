Tunnel Studio 0.21.3
4 November 2026

Restore an area from Deleted points
View one or more deleted groups, draw an area with Box, Lasso, Polygon or Brush,
then choose Restore selected points. The full-density original observations
inside that shape are restored; the remaining points stay deleted.

Ordinary deleted points return to their scan. Cleaning exclusions restore as a
separate scan, keeping the cleaned tunnel independent. The selected groups are
saved atomically and one workspace Undo reverses the complete restoration.
Cancellation and failed saves discard only incomplete restoration assets.

The deleted-point view disables ordinary delete/separate actions. Restore is
available next to Back to scan, in the Edit ribbon and in its right-click menu.
Back to scan refreshes the current retained points. Remaining deleted-point
lists are saved with the project and stay protected by storage reference checks.

The bolt-selection instruction now says to face the wall from inside the tunnel
and click a bolt head or fitting sticking out, with surrounding wall visible.
The source-bundle preservation fix from 0.21.2 remains included.

Whole-cloud bolt pockets and raised fittings
Bolts & pockets now offers Whole retained cloud and Near a picked point.
Whole cloud uses the existing streamed lining engine and bounded overlapping
windows, without the local tool's one-million-point neighbourhood admission cap.
An active source limit box still applies and is explained in the scope help.
The local scope retains its explicit working budget and seed requirement.

Recessed bolt pocket means a dip behind the neighbouring wall; the bolt head may
remain below the wall. Raised fitting means a bump extending into the tunnel.
Separate depth/height wording, thresholds and signed geometry checks support
both cases. Whole-cloud detection does not need a camera-facing seed. It labels
all retained original IDs; display and reference calibration samples stay bounded.
Detected point groups can be inspected in detail and removed reversibly, keeping
raw XYZ and attributes unchanged. Candidate centres are guides, not the selection.
No hardware identity or survey accuracy is claimed. No missing wall is invented;
reconstruction remains a separate mesh step with estimated triangles.

All lining departures and point separation
Above / below lining selects every supported measured point outside a user band
on either side, with no bolt-diameter or compactness restriction. The whole-cloud
route streams all IDs against the supported tunnel reference; a local picked
patch uses a robust quadratic lining fit. Review the groups, then Remove or
Separate selected points to a new scan. Separation and removal preserve source
coordinates and support one workspace Undo. Unsupported areas remain retained.

Export selected deleted areas
Draw an area in Deleted points and choose Export selected deleted points, or use
the Explorer Export button. The captured deleted groups export at full density
without restoring them. The export dialog can also include original source wall
points inside the drawn area, which is useful for supplying a bolt-pocket sample.
E57, XYZ and the existing point-cloud formats use the normal destination dialog.
