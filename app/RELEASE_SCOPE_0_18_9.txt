Tunnel Studio 0.18.9 release scope

Implemented: bounded streaming point-to-plane ICP with local RANSAC increments,
PCA reference normals, cKDTree matching, proper rotations, fixed scale, explicit
review and group-aware virtual-pose acceptance. Optional attributes are skipped
for geometry fitting. Source and mask revisions are checked before acceptance.
One accepted operation creates one Workspace Undo checkpoint; save failures
restore metadata. Cancellation leaves original point caches unchanged.

Sampling limits (moving/reference): Fast 25,000/50,000; Balanced 75,000/150,000;
High 150,000/300,000. All retained points are streamed to form the samples;
initial read time remains proportional to source size. Display crops are not
used as solve boundaries. At least 500 distinct retained correspondences,
adequate six-axis constraints and convergence are required. RANSAC is local,
not a global feature search. Plane RMS is not a probability or survey accuracy.

Implemented: read-only RTC360 metadata inspector (4 MiB per file), observed XML
SetupPosition parser, explicit initial-pose review/apply workflow, provenance,
tilt compensation, prior-registration protection, idempotence and undo.
Observed input: 16 records in user-supplied 231023-SC-D1 SetupPositions; the
actual scanner data is not included in the installer. project.rtc360 may be an
empty marker. Unknown schemas, mismatched IDs and invalid rotations are refused.
The initial pose is independently interpreted saved scanner positioning/VIS,
not vendor-certified decoding, coordinate control or verified field accuracy.

Validation details are in VALIDATION_0_18_9.json and the separate release checks.
Linux numerical, application, real-browser and installer-structure checks do
not substitute for native Windows/WebView2/GPU execution or field comparison.

Unchanged unresolved items: missing original R0007 .attr32 data, physical
segment recognition/rendering against the research paper, and field deletion
timing/quality on the user's complete multi-scan project.
