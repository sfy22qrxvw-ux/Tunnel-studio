Tunnel Studio 0.19.0

- Joint bundle adjustment of scan poses, shared targets and sampled surfaces;
  loop candidate validation, plan/elevation review, atomic Apply and Undo.
- Removed the former 30-scan/2,000-target-pair restriction in favour of explicit
  sample and sparse residual budgets. Tested beyond the old boundary.
- RTC360 review defaults to all raw setups, separates positioned/protected/
  unavailable statuses, reveals their 3D positions and opens cloud refinement.
- Readable raw setup labels preserve source names, stable IDs and custom names.
- Bounded parallel noise tiles, resource-aware native CPU workers, compiled
  mesh connectivity/smoothing and batched GuSAC normal queries.
- Process/memory counter failures no longer interrupt resource-aware compute.
- Saved monitoring epochs, displacement/settlement/convergence trends, baseline
  and gap handling, CSV/chart export and printable reports.
- Existing registration, selection, deletion, results and theme fixes retained.

No GPU meshing engine, automatic full-job unlinked registration, survey-accuracy
certification, missing-attribute reconstruction or paper-exact segmentation is
claimed. Detailed implementation boundaries are in RELEASE_SCOPE_0_19_0.txt.
