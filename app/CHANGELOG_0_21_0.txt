Tunnel Studio 0.21.0
4 November 2026

Meshing > Reconstruct bolt gaps
Inspect a tunnel lining mesh with open bolt footprints and create a repaired
copy. The default opening diameter is 50–150 mm. Each accepted opening uses
its surrounding wall to fit a local curved patch. New faces carry an estimated
flag and the repair records their ranges and fit residuals. A recess already
triangulated as a dent has no opening to repair. Keep the actual bolt gaps
open during initial surface creation if this repair is needed.

Measure > Tunnel volume
Select the lining mesh and the alignment, then enter or pick the start and end
chainages. The tool cuts the mesh at perpendicular section planes, creates
temporary end caps and validates the resulting shell. The result includes
enclosed tunnel volume in cubic metres and the selected reach. A wall opening,
branched portal or inconsistent solid blocks the measurement.

Analyse > Section convergence
Fit a circle and ellipse to scan cross-sections at chosen chainages. Select
a second survey epoch in the same checked datum to compare fitted diameters
and centres. Positive closure means shortening. Angles are measured from local
right towards the crown. Inadequately observed stations are identified in the
report. The workflow follows the PCA, outlier review and circle-fitting ideas
in Shen et al. (2021), https://doi.org/10.11947/j.JGGS.2021.0206. Ellipse fits
and comparisons in a common alignment frame are Tunnel Studio adaptations.

3D point display
Point tiles outside the camera view can be skipped. In a wider view, distant
tiles draw a stable subset. Point size also responds to distance. The spatial
index is built in cancellable slices as a preview arrives.

Lining analysis > Segments
Between two detected ring boundaries, the selected panel layout can supply
missing angular divisions. Measured longitudinal joints anchor the layout
where available. When no side seams are available, the offset is measured from
the fitted local frame's first radial axis towards its second. Review the
sector count and offset against the installed ring design. Set the count to
zero to use only measured boundaries. Confidence scores describe the evidence
at the boundaries of each panel.

  High (3)    Both ring ends and both side joints are supported by observations.
  Medium (2)  Both ring ends and one side joint have measured support.
  Low (1)     The ring ends have measured support. The two sides are inferred.

The review provides a confidence filter, vivid panel colours, boundary guides,
panel numbers and a summary of occupied panels and ring intervals.

Save As > Save portable copy in...
Choose a destination for a complete .tstudio copy. The save and storage dialogs
show the named project record, the working data folder and free space on its
drive. The desktop can open the working data folder in Explorer. Ordinary
named saves continue to share the existing working caches.
