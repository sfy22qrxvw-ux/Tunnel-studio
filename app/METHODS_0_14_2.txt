MIDPOINT AND FACE PLANE
Hover acquisition waits 350 ms over a supported geometry snap. Two distinct
acquired points give a + (b - a)/2 in stored world coordinates. The drawing
command has not placed a point until the user clicks or enters coordinates.
When both points lie on a common planar face of the same model, coplanar
triangles are collected and the plane is inferred from the actual geometry.
If a shared edge belongs to several planes, the most view-facing plane is
highlighted. A body diagonal or unrelated points does not define a face.
The detected frame is captured for the command; preview and accepted circle,
arc or rectangle use that frame. Off-plane snapping is blocked after capture.
World/UCS coordinates of the existing geometry are unchanged. Tracking is reset
on cancellation/new commands; camera navigation cancels a pending hover dwell.

VIEW SEPARATION
Clean, Edit and Analyse suppress editable CAD rendering and picking. Model
suppresses scan/reference rendering by default. Cloud reference explicitly
allows combined viewing, and Work in Model mode from a cloud enables it.
The views share one project coordinate system and storage but have separate
camera states. Hidden model selection is excluded from generic highlighted
export/delete actions. Import/export between bundles/projects remains explicit.

STANDARD EXTRACTION
The existing tunnel fit and default classification remain unchanged. This
continues to fit one approximately circular tunnel branch with supported local
radial profiles, including flat floors; unsupported areas remain uncertain.
The stronger behavior is opt-in. It is not applied silently to older settings.

MORE THOROUGH INTERIOR CLEANUP
1. Reuse the standard bounded spatial calibration and fitted section frames.
2. Build an additional angular profile with equal weight per longitudinal
   section, requiring at least three populated section cells per angular bin.
   This reduces domination by a dense local foreground remnant.
3. Within fitted sections, flag additional points near the inner lining only
   when both the local fit and the section-balanced reference put them inward
   beyond the keep band. The effective band is at least the requested value,
   half the voxel diagonal, plus an angular-profile variation allowance where
   necessary. Flat/sloping profiles need this guard against quantization error.
4. Deep interior uncertain points may be assessed across small support gaps
   (up to four station/angle cells beyond standard support), with at least
   200 mm or twice the standard tolerance clearance. The section-balanced
   reference can also flag supported deep inward departures of at least
   150 mm or twice the standard tolerance. Long gaps between fitted sections
   are not bridged. An opening or missing outline does not supply wall evidence.
5. Preserve original IDs and XYZ. Extra suggestions are label 4, named
   Extra interior candidates · review. Review choices govern final retention.

The default minimum inward band is 30 mm; it is a lower bound, not a claim of
30 mm surface accuracy. At default 50 mm calibration spacing the voxel guard
is about 43.3 mm, increased further on steep radial profiles. Real fittings,
joints, protrusions and rough lining may be candidates. Points sufficiently
close to the wall can remain; the algorithm cannot promise complete removal.
Standard keep labels remain lining and uncertain areas, subject to explicit
review overrides. No density-only rule deletes sparse wall behind pipes.

REFLECTION REVIEW
This is an independent conservative geometric candidate detector, not a
waveform classifier or a validated detector for every reflection type.
Calibration uses at most 140,000 deterministic spatially sampled actual points.
The supported angular reference is balanced over longitudinal sections.
A point must lie outside supported lining by the configured departure (100 mm
default), then satisfy at least two additional cues:
- Approximate radial mirroring matches at least three interior measured points
  within the geometry-match distance, with measured wall support at its foot.
- A ray from a known scanner position crosses supported measured lining before
  reaching the point; crossing is found with 14 bisection iterations and needs
  at least three nearby wall samples.
- Available intensity is outside six robust scale units from the median. Scale
  uses median absolute deviation and a central-percentile spread; constant or
  missing intensity disables this cue. Sampling is bounded and deterministic
  by original point ID. Intensity alone never rejects a point.
Known acquisition poses are used only when the source metadata establishes
one; a bounding-box centre is never substituted. Visibility evidence is disabled
when the supplied scanner position is not inside supported fitted lining.

All selected retained points are classified, in chunks of up to 25,000 for the
reflection evidence calculation, with cancellation checks. Label 1 is a review
candidate; labels 0, 2 (weak evidence) and 3 (uncertain support) are retained.
Reports record available evidence and the settings. Selection/deletion masks
and full-density source coordinates/attributes are preserved. Failed jobs do
not replace the previous saved result. Review is required before applying.

This radial approximation can miss floor/equipment reflections, curved or
specular surfaces and areas with inadequate wall support. Real recesses,
openings and outer structures can resemble ghosts. No waveform/dual-return
classification or reflection-corrected replacement points are produced.
Research background: Zhao, Yang and Schwertfeger, Mapping with Reflection,
SSRR 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12483 . That work's classifier and reported
accuracy have not been reproduced or claimed by this implementation.

VALIDATION SCOPE
See tests/validation_0_14_2.json and its individual logs/reports. Tests use
constructed geometry with known labels, the production UI in controlled API
fixtures, and real Chromium/WebGL against the modelling backend. They include
survey-sized coordinate offsets, exact midpoint/face-plane results, distinct
render/pick layers, noisy rotated floors, sparse wall, floating interior
clusters, known mirrored ghosts, dark wall and genuine outer recesses.
Two user output screenshots from the tunnel lining cleaning chat informed the
remnant patterns. Test XYZ was constructed independently: no survey coordinates
or ground-truth labels were recovered from image pixels. A customer point-cloud
sample is needed to measure performance on those exact outputs. Reported
synthetic frame timings are not measurements of the user's Windows GPU/project.
