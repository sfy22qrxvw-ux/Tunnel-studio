TUNNEL STUDIO 0.13.8 - TARGET SEARCH AND DISPLAY METHODS
Copyright (c) 2026 Damian Gulczynski

PURPOSE
Estimate the intersection of four alternating black-and-white quadrants.
No physical target size is requested. An internal support scale is a fitting
window, not an estimate of the printed target's outer dimensions.

SEARCH
1. Visit every finite, retained original XYZ record. Intensity and RGB
   brightness are processed separately. Excluded points are not used.
2. Accumulate local cell counts, intensity moments and range at support
   scales 0.05, 0.08, 0.125, 0.20, 0.32, 0.50, 0.80 and 1.25 m. Cell size
   is 0.42 times the support scale. Keep spatially distributed proposals.
   Channel-normalised scores avoid 8-bit RGB dominating normalised intensity.
3. Up to 900 local patterns proceed. The limit is reported when reached.
   Full-density traversal is not exhaustive fitting of every possible centre.
   Chunk/local quotas can omit targets; point order/density can affect proposals.
4. Collect independent overlapping original-point neighbourhoods, with a
   deterministic priority sample and a total budget near 2.4 million samples.
   Per-patch sample: 2,500 to 35,000 points. Final fit: at most 30,000 points.
5. Fit a local plane, project into it, spatially balance intensity samples,
   normalise local contrast and search crossing locations and orientations.
   The original values are not altered. Refine a soft four-quadrant model
   using robust least squares. Report the fitted continuous 3D intersection.
6. Validate the fixed pattern on all original retained points in its local
   neighbourhood, including four-quadrant support, contrast, sign agreement,
   plane support, and competing translated/rotated edges. Contrast score is
   not a probability, centre precision, metrological uncertainty or approval.
7. A weak pattern needs a consistent centre at another validated scale.
   A strong pattern (score at least 0.75) may pass at a single scale.
   These empirical thresholds reduce false positives; they can miss dirty,
   sparse, occluded or unusual targets and require real-scene validation.

PICK & FIT
Uses the same automatic scale family around the approximate clicked point,
with original-density validation. The clicked point is a seed, never an
unverified replacement for a fitted centre. No physical dimension is needed.

DISPLAY
The overview is still bounded to at most 1,000,000 displayed points/vertices.
Automatic detail activates on zoom (scale below 25% of visible scan extent),
after 450 ms without another wheel/release event. It streams original points
inside the orthographic camera volume, including a 15% lateral margin, then
uses deterministic sampling only if that region exceeds the display budget.
Deleted/hidden/isolated points and enabled limit boxes remain respected.
This is an on-demand region read, not a persistent octree/LOD index.
Large scans or slow disks may take longer; all points are not loaded into GPU
memory at once. Fit resets to the global overview. Navigation during loading
preserves the latest camera, and obsolete detail responses are discarded.
Local intensity percentiles improve contrast. User contrast/gamma are GPU
display transforms, disabled for cleaning/analysis colour overlays. Target
detection uses unmodified source intensity/RGB, independent of these sliders.

RESEARCH REVIEWED
Wang, C. and Zha, B. (2023), Point Cloud Target Registration Algorithm Based
on Local Feature Description, J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 2478, 062024.
DOI: 10.1088/1742-6596/2478/6/062024 (user-supplied paper).
Its local descriptors use normal angles, projection distances and density
for correspondence / RANSAC / ICP between 3D objects. It does not provide a
black-and-white printed-quadrant centre detector; that descriptor pipeline
has not been claimed or implemented in this update.

Rachakonda, P., Muralikrishnan, B. and Sawyer, D. (2018), Metrological
evaluation of contrast target center algorithm for terrestrial laser scanners.
https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=925952
This paper supports combining geometric and intensity data for contrast
target centres. It starts from target ROIs, uses image/edge processing and
subsequent refinement. This update is its own plane / contrast-model method,
not a reproduction or validation of NIST's spherical-domain procedure.

REAL-SCAN LIMIT
The supplied E57 has 10,125,420 points and intensity/RGB. Original-cache
hashes were unchanged after display tests. Several earlier suggestions were
visually inspected and proved to be seams; final automatic search returned
zero suggestions. No labelled Leica target in that E57 has been established,
so recall, false-negative rate and real-centre accuracy remain unverified.
Separate Leica database target records were inspected, but correspondence
and frame agreement with this E57 have not been established. No transform or
target coordinates from those databases were assumed to be E57 ground truth.
