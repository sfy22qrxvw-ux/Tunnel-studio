TUNNEL STUDIO 0.14.3 — LINING FEATURES

Model ribbon > Lining features:
- Bolt holes: compact recess candidates and projected 3D centres.
- Non-lining: separate interior/outside candidates; preserve recess allowance.
- Seams: circumferential ring seams and longitudinal between-segment seams,
  including tilted rings and sloping joints where sufficiently supported.
- Segments: reviewable regions bounded by detected seams.
Setup also offers All lining features.

Choose Fast, Detailed or More detailed. These change map resolution,
calibration size and robust fitting effort. All original selected points are
labelled. Finer detail takes longer and is not a measurement-accuracy promise.

Named results support search, highlight, isolate and full-density highlighted
export. Distinct colours identify groups; magenta indicates selection and red
indicates exclusions. CSV/JSON reports contain locations and support information.
Bolt markers and seam guides are available in Geometry & references.

Start with a short, well-scanned section showing both walls and crown. Detection
retains points by default; Non-lining proposes exclusions requiring review.
Missing seams can leave merged segment candidates. Local station is not design
chainage. The existing Extract tunnel surface behaviour remains unchanged.

Three distinct supplied research papers informed this independent geometric
implementation. No trained neural model or project-specific bolt template is
claimed. Read METHODS_0_14_3.txt for algorithms, thresholds, limits and tests.
This cumulative update retains the previous modelling and cleaning fixes.
