TUNNEL STUDIO 0.11.0 — METHODS AND FORMULA PROVENANCE
All geometric input coordinates are in metres. Vertices are stored as float64.
Temporary local centring is restored before writing a result or exporting OBJ.
Each face contains exactly three integer vertex indices. Degenerate and duplicate
faces are removed; invalid indices stop the operation.

1 — PLANAR DELAUNAY
Centred points q_i = p_i - mean(p). SVD supplies two principal in-plane axes.
Project to u_i = [q_i·e_1, q_i·e_2] and Delaunay-triangulate this 2D set.
Faces keep the measured 3D vertex coordinates. Duplicate projected points are
filtered; a maximum 3D edge-length filter avoids overly long connections.
Suitable for local near-planar surfaces, not an entire tube or folded surface.

2 — PERIODIC CYLINDRICAL DELAUNAY
Estimate a tube frame with PCA and the similarity of the two radial variances.
For local (s,y,z), theta = atan2(z,y). Aggregate occupied axial/angular cells.
Triangulate (s, r*theta), including copies offset by ±2*pi*r. Retain the central
revolution and reject excessive angular spans and long 3D edges.
This bridges ordinary irregular point sampling and handles the angular seam;
it is not a reconstruction of an arbitrary branching network.

3 — THREE-DIMENSIONAL ALPHA SHAPE
Compute Delaunay tetrahedra. For a tetrahedron p_0,...,p_3, define d_i=p_i-p_0.
Solve 2*[d_1;d_2;d_3]*c = [|d_1|²,|d_2|²,|d_3|²]. Its circumradius R=|c|.
Retain non-degenerate tetrahedra with R <= alpha. A triangle appearing in only
one retained tetrahedron is a boundary face, oriented away from that tetrahedron.
Alpha is a radius in metres here, not a squared radius. Alpha=0 chooses an initial
75th-percentile circumradius estimate; that heuristic requires visual review.
A larger alpha generally joins more of the volume and can fill real openings.

4 — SCREENED POISSON (OPEN3D)
Normals are estimated with local neighbours and oriented consistently.
The underlying Poisson field equation is Laplacian(chi)=divergence(V), where V
is derived from oriented normals. Screened Poisson adds a point interpolation
constraint; Open3D performs the reconstruction and extracts triangle faces.
Depth is limited to 5–10 for this integration; higher depth increases memory.
No density trimming is silently applied. Extrapolated regions need inspection.

5 — BALL PIVOTING (OPEN3D)
A ball of radius rho pivots about existing edges to connect oriented samples
into triangles. The implementation estimates normals consistently and uses
radii [rho, 2*rho]. Automatic rho uses spacing measured against the full working
cloud, rather than an artificially thinned neighbourhood tree.

6 — MARCHING CUBES (SCIKIT-IMAGE)
An occupancy voxel field is smoothed and an isosurface is extracted. Linear
edge interpolation uses t=(iso-f_0)/(f_1-f_0) between voxel samples.
This application's field is not a signed-distance field; scanning only a wall
can produce an occupancy shell with thickness. Voxel size is an approximation.

7 — TAUBIN SMOOTHING AND QUADRIC REDUCTION
Uniform graph Laplacian: L(v_i) = mean(neighbours(v_i)) - v_i.
Each Taubin iteration applies v <- v + 0.5*L(v), then v <- v - 0.53*L(v).
Both passes recompute the Laplacian. This reduces ordinary smoothing shrinkage;
it does not guarantee zero shrinkage or survey accuracy.
Open3D quadric decimation collapses edges using plane-distance error quadrics.
For homogeneous plane pi=(a,b,c,d), Q is formed from sums of pi*pi^T and vertex
cost is [x,y,z,1]*Q*[x,y,z,1]^T. The requested triangle count is a target.

8 — SURFACE SAMPLING AND FIT DIAGNOSTICS
Triangle area A = |(b-a) cross (c-a)|/2. Choose triangles with probability
A_i/sum(A). Uniform barycentric sampling then draws points over their interiors.
For working source subset P and sampled surface S:
CD² = 0.5 * [mean_p(min_s |p-s|²) + mean_s(min_p |s-p|²)].
Units: m². This is normalized squared Chamfer with a fixed random seed.
Separately, mesh-sample-to-cloud RMS = sqrt(mean_s(min_p |s-p|²)), using all
working cloud points in this second metric. P95 is also retained in metadata.
These are approximate sampled point-set distances, not exact point-to-triangle
surface distances, full original-cloud validation, signed errors or acceptance
criteria. Working-point and surface-sample counts are recorded.

9 — TOPOLOGY AND HOLE CLOSURE
Count undirected occurrences of each mesh edge. One occurrence is a boundary;
more than two is a non-manifold edge. Surface area sums triangle areas.
Ear clipping attempts simple boundary loops that are nearly planar and no longer
than 2,000 vertices; concave planar loops are triangulated without a centre fan.
Skipped loops remain open. Edge counts do not detect every non-manifold vertex,
self-intersection or overlapping triangle, and do not certify watertightness.
