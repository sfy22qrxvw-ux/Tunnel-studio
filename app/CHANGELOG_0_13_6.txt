TUNNEL STUDIO 0.13.6
Clear cleaning groups, an interactive limit box and a section locator
13 October 2026

LIMIT BOX: DOUBLE-CLICK A CORNER TO EDIT
Double-click any of the eight corners to open the box editor. It stays beside
the main viewport, so the scan can still be rotated, panned and zoomed.

White square corners resize the box with the opposite corner fixed. Cyan
circle handles move one face while keeping the opposite face fixed. Orange
diamond handles on the edges rotate the box about its centre. Use edges in
different directions to rotate about all three box axes; hold Shift for
15-degree steps. A face looking directly towards the camera can be moved
in depth by dragging its handle vertically. The centre and sizes can also
be entered numerically without losing the box's current rotation.

Hide outside is a checkbox beside Limit box and in its editor. It is off for
a new box. On shows only the inside of the box and keeps point editing and
cleaning inside it. Off restores the entire scan and editing scope without
removing the box. The outline updates during dragging; point clipping updates
on release, avoiding a full-density reload for every mouse movement.

Save box / done or Escape finishes editing. Double-click a corner to resume.
Escape during a drag cancels that movement. Use Remove box to discard it.
The box applies to the visible scan items, not mesh models. Existing saved
boxes retain their prior clipping behaviour until Hide outside is changed.

The backend stores centre, three perpendicular axes and three dimensions.
Preview, cleaning, copying, separating, deleting and scan sections use the
same rotated boundary, preserving the original survey coordinates and
attributes. Turning Hide outside off removes that editing restriction.

CROSS-SECTIONS: DRAG THE CUTTING LINE
Cross-section now shows the selected scan in a top view on the left and the
computed cross-section on the right. Drag the yellow line or click the scan
to choose a position. The line and position number update immediately; the
full-density section is calculated once you release. The yellow band shows
the chosen thickness. A range slider, numeric position and arrow keys provide
fine adjustment. No alignment is needed. Horizontal cuts use a labelled side
view so you can move the slice up and down.

Drag the title bar to move the cross-section window, or its bottom-right corner
to resize it. Reset position restores its default position and size. The limit
box and estimated-alignment windows can be moved in the same way.

The locator is a bounded scan overview (up to 40,000 displayed points), while
the section and CSV use retained full-density points. The cutting frame stays
fixed between slices, avoiding a new direction estimate or preliminary full
scan pass each time the line moves. Current selection and active limit-box
restrictions still apply. Old results are visibly dimmed while the position
changes, and the CSV link becomes available after a successful calculation.

CLEANING: TWO CLEAR GROUPS
Review now shows green Tunnel / keep and red Noise / remove, with full-density
point totals. Tunnel only, Noise only and Both change visibility without
changing which points are kept. Selected pieces remain magenta and retain a
separate result-group badge, so selection cannot be confused with membership.

Individual pieces are inside an optional Review individual pieces panel.
They are parts of the same result, not separate files. Keep as tunnel and
Mark as noise / remove let you correct the proposed membership. The method
explanation states what the chosen cleaner does; noise cleaning does not
automatically identify every object or establish which surface is the lining.

Create one tunnel scan combines all green points into one scan named
"Tunnel · <source>". Hide source after creating is optional and checked by
default, preventing the source and result from obscuring one another. Other
scans are left alone. The scan list identifies the result with a green badge.
The two exports are named Tunnel_Studio_Tunnel_Kept and
Tunnel_Studio_Noise_Removed, with the chosen point-cloud extension.
