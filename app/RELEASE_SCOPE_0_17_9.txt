Tunnel Studio 0.17.9 adds project-level storage management to Open Project.
The objective is to let users permanently remove obsolete projects and release their exclusive caches safely without manual deletion from %LOCALAPPDATA%\TunnelStudio.

Deletion safety:
- other named projects protect shared files;
- the current project protects its files unless it is the selected project being deleted;
- startup recovery snapshots protect their referenced files;
- review reports exclusive and shared/protected bytes before confirmation;
- deletion stages exclusive assets on the same volume and rolls back staging failures;
- orphan cleanup only considers known Tunnel Studio generated cache/result patterns not referenced by live project/recovery metadata.
