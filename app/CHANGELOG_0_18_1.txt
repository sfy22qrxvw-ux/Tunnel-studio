Tunnel Studio 0.18.1 - research audit and consistent section baselines
30 October 2026

Saved section comparisons now enforce matching wall band, design radius and
fit threshold as well as plane and slice width. Invalid or incomplete saved
settings are rejected before fitting, with clear restoration/recreation guidance.
Different survey epochs and masks remain supported; original coordinates stay
unchanged. A true centre translation remains visible in comparison results.

Added a six-paper algorithm decision record. The Camara2024 robust section
method was already incorporated, with documented corrections. Camara2025 was
critically reviewed; its printed filtering inequality and unconstrained
per-ring ICP are not adopted as default absolute-deformation methods.

233 application regression tests passed; nine dedicated baseline tests added.
All 0.18.0 performance, whole-project analysis, registration and storage fixes
are retained. M3C2-EP and a confidence-qualified epoch engine are not included.
See ALGORITHM_REVIEW_2026_10_30.txt and VALIDATION_0_18_1.json.
