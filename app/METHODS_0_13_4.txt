TUNNEL STUDIO 0.13.4 - SCAN GEOMETRY AND CLEANING METHODS

Coordinates are in metres. Original XYZ/attributes are not changed.

Scan section: let c be the chosen centre and n a unit normal. A point p is in
 a slice of thickness w when abs((p-c) dot n) <= w/2. Unit in-plane vectors
 u and v are perpendicular to n and each other. Plot/export coordinates are
 U=(p-c) dot u, V=(p-c) dot v. The plot uses the same scale on both axes.
 Full retained point IDs are filtered; the 60,000-point cap only affects display.

Estimated alignment (independent implementation):
1. Deterministic uniform priority sampling of at most 180,000 retained points
   in the chosen area; existing masks and the active limit box are applied.
2. A robustly centred PCA chooses the dominant direction. The greatest
   covariance eigenvalue must be at least twice the next; ambiguous geometry
   is rejected. The longest-component direction fixes a reproducible orientation.
3. Stations span the 1st-99th longitudinal percentiles at the chosen spacing.
   Each slab uses half-width max(0.25 m, 0.65 times the station interval).
4. Divide cross-section angles into 72 cells. Select up to three observations
   nearest each cell's 80th radial percentile to reduce uneven density and
   interior-object influence. Fit the resulting shell, not an imagined surface.
5. Start with an algebraic circle fit. Refine centre a and radius r by weighted
   geometric least squares on e_i=norm(q_i-a)-r. Huber weights are
   min(1,1.5*sigma/abs(e_i)), where sigma=max(0.003 m,1.4826*MAD(e)).
   Up to 12 iterations; section angular coverage must reach 210 degrees.
   Radius must be 0.15-50 m and the winsorised radial RMS no greater than
   max(0.15 m,0.12*r). These are practical screening limits, not confidence.
6. Reject unsupported gaps larger than 2.1 times the station interval and
   estimates with fewer than three supported nodes. Optional smoothing uses
   (previous+2*current+next)/4 on interior node positions; ends stay fixed.
7. Chainage increments are Euclidean distances along the node polyline.
   Fitted radius, not nominal design radius, is written to the CSV.

This method is limited to approximately circular, gently curving single
branches with sufficient lining coverage. It is not a general skeletonisation
or a geodetic design reconstruction. Do not interpret the estimate's residual
as survey accuracy. User edits are validated for finite coordinates, positive
radius and strictly increasing chainage before use.

Cleaning presets (all process every retained point in the selected area):
                       Fast     Detailed     Fine
Noise neighbours          8           12       24
Patch resolution (m)   0.30         0.20     0.10
Normal neighbours        10           15       25
Envelope angle (deg)      6            3      1.5
Other numerical settings remain as currently entered. Adjusting a numerical
control manually switches the preset selector to Custom. The preset name
expresses computational detail, not accuracy or guaranteed cleaning quality.

Small-area noise path: the radius-support and local median/MAD criteria are
unchanged. For at most min(150,000, floor(1,500,000/(neighbours+1))) selected
points, deduplicate coordinates and classify them together with one cKDTree.
This is the same neighbourhood calculation as tiled cleaning with complete
2R halos. The original point-ID mapping expands labels back to duplicates.
Larger selections retain spatial tiling. A new selected area is collected in
one bounded pass through the source and cached temporarily for the run; the
cache is removed on completion/cancellation. There is no permanent spatial
index yet, so a small selection in a very large file still requires that read.

Point edits: byte masks retain raw cache length; 0 means retained. Every edit
writes a new immutable mask. Deleted groups are identified by before/after
mask differences and can be restored independently. Separate commits its new
XYZ/attribute cache and source mask together. Undo/Redo uses both sides of
that transaction. Snapshot counts/extents are computed from retained points
and cached against the exact mask revision; original raw count is unchanged.
