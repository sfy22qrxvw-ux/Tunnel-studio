Tunnel Studio 0.11.1
- Desktop and browser fallback start with a new empty project.
- The previous non-empty autosave is preserved as a separate previous-session
  snapshot under Open before resetting the active workspace.
- Existing named projects are not overwritten by startup recovery.
- Empty startups do not create extra blank projects in the Open list.
- Raw recovery copies are retained; failed preservation stops before clearing work.
- Includes all changes from 0.11.0.
