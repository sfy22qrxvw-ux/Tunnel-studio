Tunnel Studio 0.21.5 | Local circular references in curved centreline frames

The source and unchanged Windows runtime baseline is the delivered 0.21.4.
section_circle.py is an independent mathematical circle-fit workflow. It uses
local centreline projection, robust least-squares circles and overlapping fits;
it is not a claim to reproduce a research paper or a trained semantic model.

A bounded spatial sample supplies the initial scan-derived guide, or the user
chooses the loaded project alignment. The initial guide is never written back
to project alignment data. All source points are streamed again into sparse,
disk-backed fitting cells. At most 12,000 actual representative observations
per distance bin are retained for calibration. This is not an admission cap.
Source coordinates are not replaced with fitted or voxel-centre coordinates.

The 3D guide is projected with cKDTree seeds and bounded Newton refinement.
Rotation-minimising perpendicular frames avoid a single global tunnel direction.
Short thin sections refine only the guide before the final user-length circle
fits. Existing finer guide geometry is preserved during refinement, including
bends at the ends; coarse fitting anchors do not replace it. Final circle windows
are 5 m by default at 2.5 m spacing. Centres/radii blend between windows; an
unsupported neighbouring fit keeps the affected points unassessed.

Circle support requires at least 240 degrees of measured angular coverage,
adequate consensus, and fitted RMS no greater than 35% of the tighter user band.
Unverified/non-circular/partial sections stay unselected; missing measurements
are not reconstructed. RANSAC outlier trimming can still confuse real geometric
features, so every outside-band group remains a candidate requiring review.
No candidates are selected automatically. Inward means toward the circle centre;
outward means away from it, independently of viewing direction.

Classification checks every retained source observation in <=100k blocks,
using 64-bit IDs. Only candidate IDs/labels/review flags are stored, not another
copy of the whole cloud. Preview samples are bounded separately. Circle view
clips the display to its fitting interval and draws its reference ring; this
display does not change full-density group selection or source coordinates.

Native Windows/WebView2 and Damian's 921-million-point project are not run here.
Constructed tests check curved circular walls, tight bands, both signs, reversed
viewing direction, rotated/translated coordinates, partial/oval uncertainty,
>1 million observations, 64-bit IDs, cancellation and exact reversible edits.
These establish implementation behaviour, not field recognition/survey accuracy.
