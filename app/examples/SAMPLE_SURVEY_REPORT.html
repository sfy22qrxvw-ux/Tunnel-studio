Method and review

Inputs: registered coordinates in metres and a circular design on a polyline alignment. No coordinate transformation or scan registration is performed. Chainage uses nearest plan projection. Section coordinates use the local plane normal to the 3D alignment tangent. Positive radial deviation is outside the design.

All retained points contribute to section statistics; figures are sampled. Circle and ellipse fits use balanced 1° angular bins. Ovality = 100 × (major diameter − minor diameter) / design diameter. Fits are withheld if angular coverage is inadequate. Missing values are not zero. The ellipse uses a normalised implicit residual, not a claimed Amberg or ITA-certified algorithm. Wall-band filtering and manual exclusion can remove genuine defects.

Creep = 1000 × (measured face chainage − design face chainage), in mm. Positive is ahead. User-entered display limits do not constitute acceptance criteria. All loaded ring observations are listed, including those outside the section-report range. Validate against independently checked project results before use.
