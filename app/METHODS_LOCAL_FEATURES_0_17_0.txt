Tunnel Studio 0.17.0 — local feature selection and SMRG workload improvements

SEEDED REGION GROWTH
Pick one retained scan point. Search radius bounds a spherical local area in
project/world metre coordinates; the current point mask, hidden regions,
isolation and limit box are respected. An optional drawn selection further
restricts that area. Conservative source-block bounds reduce repeated reads;
the first use may need to build the block index from the entire source.

Every retained original point in the local area is read and checked. There is
no silent sampling or downsampling of the selection. Coincident coordinates
share a geometric calculation but every original row ID is preserved.

Local normals use the chosen number of nearest distinct measured locations.
The seed grows through exact radius neighbours whose normals agree within the
maximum surface turn and whose symmetric point-to-tangent-plane step is within
the surface step limit. Ambiguous high-curvature corners do not propagate.
Growth can follow a smoothly curving surface; it is not a semantic pipe/lining
classifier. It can join touching objects with compatible geometry. Reaching
the search-radius boundary is reported so the user can enlarge and rerun.

PROTRUDING BOLTS / FITTINGS
Look towards the lining from inside the tunnel, and pick on or near a fitting.
The view direction at the picked seed defines the positive protrusion side.
The explicit Reverse protrusion side option reverses that sign. Viewpoints
nearly tangent to the inferred surface are rejected.

A robust quadratic reference is fitted to an annulus of surrounding measured
lining, using local float64 coordinates. The fit must have enough consistent
support, acceptable residual roughness and measured support in at least six
of eight angular sectors. This allows a locally curved lining without claiming
an exact cylinder. All local original points are classified against that fit.
Reported height is the signed residual along the local reference normal axis,
not an exact shortest distance to a curved quadratic surface.

Only positive deviations above the larger of the requested minimum height and
four reference RMS values, and below maximum protrusion, enter candidates.
Negative deviations are recesses on the selected side and are not bolt heads.
Candidates must be connected at the requested connection distance, have the
minimum original-point count, fit within maximum diameter, and avoid strongly
elongated footprints. At least six of eight nearby surrounding support probes
must have a measured lining neighbour. The outer search band is excluded to
reduce unsupported extrapolation at the edge of the area.

These are geometric protrusion candidates. Compact debris or other fittings
can have the same shape as a bolt. This is not hardware identification, and
no field precision/recall is claimed without labelled scans. Settings need
to reflect scan spacing, noise and the size of the feature. Deep occlusions,
sharp corners, broad equipment and poorly supported patches may yield no
candidate or a rejected fit. The original measured points are never moved;
the method creates no lining behind a removed object.

REVIEW, REFINE, REMOVE, UNDO
Orange points are selected; cyan points are unselected candidates; grey points
provide measured context. The display is bounded to 60,000 local points, with
space reserved for small candidates. Display count and exact selection count
are reported separately. All removal uses the exact original signed int64 IDs,
not the displayed sample. Group selection can be trimmed by a drawn box,
polygon, or lasso. Re-selecting groups resets that trimming.

Preview, group selection and refinement do not alter the scan. Source geometry,
mask and visible-area fingerprints and preview revisions must still match
before removal. Confirmed removal uses the application's immutable exclusion
mask and existing edit history. Undo/Redo restore exact rows; project save and
export use the normal retained-point workflow. Preview caches are temporary
and need to be recalculated after switching/reopening a project.

WORKLOAD LIMITS
The local point budget can be 100,000, 250,000, 500,000 (default), or 1,000,000
original retained points. Exceeding it stops before replacing the previous
preview; reduce radius or draw a limit box. This limit is not a sample count.
An exact-neighbour graph exceeding 30,000,000 neighbour visits stops and asks
for a smaller connection distance/radius. Progress and cancellation are checked
between bounded source reads, normal batches, graph batches and fit passes.

SMRG GROUP SURFACE PATCHES
SMRG remains an independent geometric grouping method. Its largest connected
region and boundary-overlap suggestion do not identify all pipes versus lining.
GuSAC remains the primary full-scan tunnel-cleaning choice. No method is silently
switched to another method.

The recovery step previously rescanned all point labels for every cluster and
rebuilt the growing retained KD tree after every accepted cluster. Version
0.17.0 indexes cluster rows once and uses a logarithmic forest of exact KD trees.
Nearest-support distances, cluster order and overlap decisions remain the same.
The 22,500-point / 401-cluster synthetic step benchmark and equivalence tests are
included in the validation report. This is not a whole-algorithm or 550-million-
point speed benchmark and does not improve SMRG's semantic classification.

SMRG now rejects selections above 3,000,000 retained original points before
extraction. An unmasked whole-cloud request can be rejected from count metadata
without reading XYZ; masked counts use bounded mask reads. Drawn selections
are counted at full density, stopping once the limit is exceeded. The prior
review and scan remain intact. This prevents the previous unbounded whole-area
workflow from silently launching thousands of independent memory blocks.

VALIDATION BOUNDARIES
Generated labelled fixtures cover curved and rotated lining at large survey
coordinates, protrusions versus recesses, elongated pipe rejection, unsupported
fits, connected/disconnected surfaces, duplicate rows, masks, local cap failures,
stale state, cancellation, exact-ID refinement, removal, Undo/Redo, save/reopen
metadata and failed-save rollback. Existing SMRG surface tests remain unchanged.
UI event checks exercise the production panel against stateful API fixtures.
An isolated production HTTP/Chromium workflow additionally imports a generated
labelled scan through the visible ribbon and file picker, clicks measured seeds,
reviews and refines exact candidates, removes through ordinary Delete, and
verifies keyboard Undo/Redo and original source/cache identity. A separate local
selection/delete benchmark uses the supplied 10,125,420-point E57 cache and
checks exact IDs, persisted masks, Undo/Redo and unchanged original data.
These checks do not establish semantic accuracy on the real scan, performance
on the user's 550-million-point scan, native Windows display/GPU behaviour or
whole-desktop memory/refresh time on that scan. Measured timings and source
hashes are in the accompanying validation reports.
