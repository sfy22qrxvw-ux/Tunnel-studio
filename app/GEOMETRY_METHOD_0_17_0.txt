TUNNEL STUDIO 0.17.0 — GENERAL GEOMETRY

Model > Geometry contains Create plane, Fit best plane and Fit best cylinder.
Select the local measured surface first, preview the model, review its residuals,
then Add model. Preview itself does not change the survey project. Added geometry
is a normal editable named model with a visibility bulb, workspace Undo/Redo,
project persistence, DXF/OBJ/Model JSON export and construction parameters.

Create plane
Enter its centre in project/world E/N/H metres, choose horizontal, vertical or a
custom normal direction, and set its width/height. The normal is perpendicular
to the plane. The finite rectangle contains two triangles and has an analytic
plane definition. Moving or editing it does not move any measured point.

Fit best plane
All selected retained finite points contribute to a stable float64 covariance.
The smallest-eigenvalue direction gives the orthogonal least-squares normal.
A second full pass computes perpendicular RMS, mean absolute and maximum
absolute residuals, plus the finite in-plane extents. Coincident or effectively
linear selections are rejected. Substantial out-of-plane spread is reported.

Fit best cylinder
This is a general cylinder fit, independent of the tunnel alignment. A bounded
16,000-point initialization sample and three principal-axis starts estimate a
starting cylinder. A damped geometric least-squares refinement then uses every
selected retained point in bounded chunks. The parameters are two transverse
axis offsets, two axis-direction coordinates and radius. Full-data residuals
and axial extent are computed after convergence. The implementation searches
local least-squares solutions; it does not claim a guaranteed global optimum.

Planar/linear selections, insufficient axial extent, angular coverage below
approximately 45 degrees, poorly conditioned solutions, nonconvergence and RMS radial error
above 5 percent of radius are rejected. Partial arcs below 120 degrees and RMS
above 1 percent of radius receive a visible review note. These are operational
quality checks, not a calibration certificate or survey acceptance tolerance.
Angular coverage uses occupied 5-degree bins over all selected points; the
display explicitly labels it approximate. The residual calculations are not
binned and use every selected point.

The resulting cylinder is a capped analytic model. Its unobserved side and end
caps are model surfaces, not newly measured lining or repaired scan points.
Fit residuals are radial distances to the cylinder side, whereas comparison
against the saved finite solid also includes its end caps.

Selection and scale
Highlighted-point fitting uses the full 3D selection, original retained points,
current masks and visible-point filters. Choosing Entire selected scan uses all
retained points, including temporarily hidden ones; this is stated in the UI.
Display density does not reduce the fit. Selected coordinates are streamed once
to temporary disk storage as float64; subsequent fitting passes read bounded
100,000-point chunks. A billion-point selection still takes substantial disk
space and repeated processing. No 550-million-point timing or whole-desktop RAM
guarantee is asserted by this implementation.

Review and persistence
Preview carries the captured source and CAD revisions. Apply rejects it if the
source or modelling workspace changed. Cancellation and failed writes do not
commit partial models. The original source files remain unchanged. Fit evidence
stored with the object describes the original fit before later parameter edits.

Validation in this release work
The focused geometry suite has 21 unique tests: known rotated planes/cylinders
at survey coordinates; noisy and partial cylinder surfaces; full-data refinement
of points outside the initialization sample; all-point residuals; masks and
spatial selection; degenerate/noncylindrical rejection; cancellation; stale
source/CAD protection; failed-save rollback; Undo/Redo; source hashes; editable
dimensions; analytic plane compatibility; DXF/OBJ/Model JSON; and actual portable
project save/reopen with both fitted models and original scan bytes preserved.
Six separate JavaScript event tests use the production tool UI with simulated
HTTP/WebGL. They are not native Windows or GPU tests.
