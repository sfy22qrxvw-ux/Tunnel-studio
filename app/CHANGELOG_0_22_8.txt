Tunnel Studio 0.22.8
9 November 2026

Clean Tunnel
Noise classification now queries the KD-tree once per point block and
reuses the same neighbour indices and validity flags for MAD rejection.
The support-window budget includes the cached neighbours.

Analysis and point edits
Use for analysis and ring-joint suggestions can reuse a verified full-density
alignment projection and retained-point total. Ordered point IDs can bypass
the sort during reversible deletion and restoration.

Interface
Background progress requests send only job progress until completion.
Cross-section progress updates also use lightweight responses.
